01 — Why Anthropic

The role asks for two people. I'd like to be both of them.

Read the posting closely and it wants someone who can translate financial performance and strategy for sophisticated investors — and someone genuinely excited to explain AI safety research. In most candidate pools those are two different résumés. Capital markets people learn the product on the job. Product people learn the investors on the job.

I have been doing both at once, in parallel, for two years.

I run investor relations for a fund I co-founded. Not the marketing version — the actual lifecycle. I author the quarterly and annual LP reports covering portfolio performance, capital deployment, return profiles, and market environment. I raised the capital. I delivered the first cash distribution and ran the governance vote behind it, modeling distribute-versus-reinvest for 60+ investors. I manage the securities workstreams with outside counsel: accredited-investor requirements, a SAFE renegotiation with a lead investor, trademark licensing.

And I ship production software on Claude. Three enterprise-compliant AI agents, live inside a global financial institution, built and reviewed under that institution's compliance regime. I know what it takes to get a frontier model through a bank's risk committee, because I have done it — and that is the exact conversation an investor in this company will eventually want to have.

The candidate who can only do the first half will tell your investors what happened last quarter. I'd rather help them understand why the next eight quarters are worth underwriting.
