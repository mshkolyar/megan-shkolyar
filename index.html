About

Perspective

I think about AI differently than most people in strategy consulting — because I've also sat on the other side of the table, as an investor trying to figure out which AI bets actually matter.

Adoption is harder than deployment
Most AI initiatives fail not because the technology doesn't work, but because the organization isn't ready for it. The change management side of AI is the most underrated problem in enterprise tech.
Startups are the real AI laboratory
Enterprise AI moves slowly. Startups move fast and learn. Watching founders navigate the AI landscape in real time is the best signal I know.
The who matters as much as the what
Which companies get access to frontier AI, which founders get backed — these are not neutral decisions. I care deeply about expanding access and building programs that bring more people into the ecosystem.
Responsible AI is a strategy problem, not just an ethics problem
Governance, safety, and trust aren't constraints on AI deployment — they're the preconditions for it. Organizations that get this right will win.
